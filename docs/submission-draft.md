Title: IncidentDesk — Challengeable Incident Evidence Reconciliation

One-liner: A GenLayer workspace that reconciles official incident statements against frozen service scopes, with cited evidence, challengeable revisions and exact customer acknowledgement.

Description (under 1000 characters):

IncidentDesk resolves an incident-review ambiguity: a status post may concern another feature, a different time window, or a preliminary claim later corrected. Participants freeze a service scope and capture hash-bound official GitHub/Cloudflare incident updates. GenLayer validators independently interpret scope, dates and impact, compare semantic fields, and substantively check exact source citations. Code reconciles the findings into immutable, digest-linked revisions. A challenge requires new official evidence; only the designated customer can acknowledge the exact current revision. The UI supports source inspection, revision history, real wallet writes, receipt verification and pending-transaction recovery. The example distinguishes delayed log visibility from workflow execution impact. All demo actors and scenarios are project-controlled. Official operator statements are not measured uptime, customer telemetry, SLA compliance or compensation evidence. No funds are handled.

5. Verify the customer acknowledgement binds to revision 2's exact digest. Follow the source and contract links to reproduce the record.
6. Switch to Live contract and connect MetaMask on Studionet. Open a separate synthetic case, capture official evidence, evaluate, challenge and acknowledge.

Expected verification: The recorded case has two immutable revisions, a resolved challenge, two frozen official incident sources, and an exact customer acknowledgement of revision 2. The recorded public receipts show successful full-validator execution.
